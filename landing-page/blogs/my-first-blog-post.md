# Sui Sentinel: Securing AI for the Web3 Frontier

AI is moving fast. Too fast, some might say.

Every week, we see new Web3 projects rush to integrate AI models, autonomous trading agents, and oracles that interpret real world events. It is exciting. But here is the uncomfortable truth: **today’s AI is fragile**.

You don’t need a PhD to jailbreak an LLM. A carefully crafted prompt can make even the most “aligned” model spew harmful content, leak private data, or execute unintended actions. If your smart contract relies on an AI agent to make decisions, what happens when that agent is tricked?

That is where **Sui Sentinel** comes in.

---

## Built for the AI and Web3 Frontier

**Sui Sentinel** is the first **gamified AI security platform** built on the Sui blockchain. It is designed to **stress-test, audit, and harden AI systems before they go live in production**.

Think of it like a **bug bounty for AI prompts**.

- Defenders deploy a **Sentinel**, an AI agent with locked funds and clear instructions.
- Attackers try to steal those funds by tricking the AI through messages rather than exploiting code.
- Every attack attempt costs money.
  - **50%** boosts the prize pool
  - **40%** rewards the Sentinel creator
  - **10%** sustains the protocol
- If your Sentinel survives **14 days without being breached**, you walk away with your capital and all the fees earned.

---

## Two Ways to Secure Your AI

### 1. Use Our Battle Tested Model

Sui Sentinel runs a **custom trained model** that is continuously stress tested and improved through live attacks on the platform. The more people attack and defend, the stronger it becomes.

For teams integrating AI into their product, this offers a **ready to use, security hardened alternative** without heavy research and development costs.

---

### 2. Bring Your Own Model

Already have your own AI model? **You can connect it directly.**

Deploy a Sentinel using your own model’s API endpoint and let the community attempt to break it for profit.

- If they succeed, it is a clear warning that your system is not ready.
- If it holds strong, you gain **real world validation from skilled attackers in Web3**.

## Built on Trust with TEE, Nautilus, and Sui

Under the hood, Sui Sentinel uses **Nautilus by Mysten Labs** and **Trusted Execution Environments** such as **AWS Nitro Enclaves**.

- Every AI decision runs inside a **hardware secured enclave**
- Every response is **cryptographically signed using ed25519**
- Responses are verified **on chain by Move smart contracts**

**No blind trust. No hidden logic. Only verifiable and tamper resistant AI behavior.**
